What changed:

  • Explain the funds. The purpose of each fund is clearly explained using blurbs extracted from UBC pages.
  • One element per screen. The pattern GDS adopted as its default for public services after research showed it helps low-confidence users and works better on mobile.
  • Wallets before the form. Apple Pay and Google Pay supply name, email and billing address in one authentication. Engaging Networks supports both natively through Stripe.
  • Fields cut from 16 to 9. Title, address type, address line 2, telephone, cardholder name and relationship-to-UBC all removed or moved after the gift.
  • The progress bar starts partly filled. The goal-gradient effect: people accelerate when a target looks close.
  • Need for an address is explained. CRA requires it on the receipt, and explaining it reduces resistance.
  • Monthly is prominent but never pre-selected. Pre-checked recurring boxes increase revenue but generate refunds and regulatory attention.
  • One expiry field, not two dropdowns. Two selects is roughly six taps; a formatted text input is four keystrokes.
  • Autofill preserved. Correct autocomplete attributes are used throughout to support autofill.
  • Accessibility. Focus moves to each step heading, changes are announced to screen readers, motion respects the reduced-motion setting, and every selection is at least 48px.
